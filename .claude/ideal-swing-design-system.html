01 / Foundation

Design principles

The system should feel like a modern performance instrument. Avoid playful softness, excessive pill shapes, oversized shadows, and generic dashboard styling.

01

Performance first

Scores, progress, timing, and coaching feedback should dominate before decoration. Every accent should carry meaning.

02

Strong geometry

Use 6 to 14px corners for most UI. Reserve circles for recording, scores, markers, and motion-related controls.

03

Motion through aqua

Aqua marks action, improvement, trajectory, and movement. Cobalt identifies selected, primary, or authoritative states.

04

Dense, not crowded

Use narrow display type, compact metadata, and strong alignment to fit useful golf data without making the screen feel busy.

02 / Color

11 / Dark Mode

Night performance

Dark mode uses deep blue-black surfaces rather than pure black. Accent color becomes more selective, while surface contrast, spacing, and tonal blocks carry the structure without borders.

Dark mode is a native theme, not an inversion.

Keep background layers visibly separated, preserve the navy identity, and let aqua stay luminous without overusing glow. The top-right theme control switches this entire design system page between both production palettes.

12 / Usage

Guardrails

A small set of rules keeps new screens recognizably Ideal Swing even when they introduce new metrics, workflows, and analysis tools.

Do
  • Use cobalt for selected and primary states.
  • Use aqua for action, trajectory, motion, and improvement.
  • Keep most corners between 6px and 14px.
  • Use condensed heavy type for scores and headings.
  • Use a single dominant performance card per screen.
Avoid
  • Turning every control into a pill.
  • Using green or red as major surface colors.
  • Adding heavy glow to every metric.
  • Mixing multiple unrelated gauge styles in one small panel.
  • Making dark mode pure black with neon accents everywhere.
